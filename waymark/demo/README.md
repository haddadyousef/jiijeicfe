# Waymark demo route (P5)

Generated by `py -m wm_pipeline.run demo --pick 1` from the real Project Sidewalk street geometry and the real priors bundle.
**A shortlist, not a walked route.** A person must look at it on a map and walk it before it is the demo.

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `route.json` | The app's route format (`M_NOTES.md` 1.1): waypoints, `lon` not `lng`, crossings marked `crossing: true`. Load with `CANEKIT_ROUTE_FILE`. |
| `route_trace.json` | GPS replay trace (`M_NOTES.md` 1.2), a point every 5 m, for `ios/scripts/e2e.py --trace`. |
| `route.gpx` | The same track as GPX 1.1, for Xcode location simulation and viewing. |
| `test_priors.json` | **SYNTHETIC** labels (ids from 9000001000) planted along this route for the e2e replay, through `WAYMARK_PRIORS_FILE`. Never shipped. One `surface_problem` has no validations: one spotter "not there" tap drops it below tau (D-31). |
| `route_ranking.md` / `.json` | The ranked shortlist and why (score rule in `routes.score_route`). |
| `top3.geojson` | The top three paths, for viewing on a map. |

## The chosen route (Yousef, 15:32 PT)

`The Ave and NE 42nd to The Ave and NE 50th`: 727 m, straight north along University Way NE, 9 waypoints, 3 crossings.
The other shortlist routes were dropped: rank 1 was a C-shaped detour that exists only to pass two stale labels, rank 2 included a long straight
segment I could not confirm is walkable. On this route the real priors hold 1 obstacle, 2 surface problems and 47 curb ramps (no stale-candidate
label and no crosswalk labels), so the live walk is a thin demo of warnings. **The "memory learned" moment comes from the replay with the SYNTHETIC
`test_priors.json`, always labelled as simulated** (demo honesty rules).

## Caveats

- Street geometry is the street **centreline**. Sidewalks run beside it, so waypoints can be a few metres off the real walking line.
- The street data has no campus footpaths (only residential, tertiary, secondary and primary roads), so every route is on streets in the U District.
- Crossings are inferred, not surveyed: a crosswalk label, or three or more curb-ramp labels within 20 m (intersection corners), on the route. Check them on the walk.
- Start and end are approximate landmark positions snapped to the nearest street node.
- To regenerate: `py -m wm_pipeline.run demo --pick 3` (rank numbers change if the data or the score rule changes; check `route_ranking.md`).
